Background:
  Lesaka Technologies is a South African fintech company which offers various banking and financial security services. They offer a software as a third party which can be used to manage transactions for individuals and merchants. They also offer security for various utilities such as water billing and metering services for enterprises to maintain business growth and proceedings.

  
Business Model Brief:
  Lesaka offers many services to various different organisations and individuals.
  They offer banking and transaction management to individuals. They offer various payout and security solutions to merchants. They also offer utility and metering services to various enterprises. 
  This organisation promotes financial security for all kinds of individuals and business owners.
